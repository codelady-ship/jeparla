Clean-up of the seller's own photo of the jewelry item to the catalog standard. Do not generate, redraw or
retouch the item: its shape, metal colour, stones, engraving, scratches and proportions stay exactly as
photographed. Only the surroundings change.
1. Remove the background around the item precisely, keeping thin chains, prongs and gaps.
2. Place the item on a plain warm cream background (#F7F3EA), centred, filling about 70% of the frame.
3. Add a soft, neutral contact shadow below the item; no reflections, props, text, logos or watermarks.
4. Correct white balance only so that the background is neutral cream; do not change the item's colours.
5. Crop to 4:5 portrait.
